Three Number Programs

Enter a whole number and calculate its factorial, the sum from 1 to N, or the separate sums of odd and even numbers.

01

Factorial (N!)

Multiply all whole numbers from 1 through N. By definition, 0! = 1; negative factorials are unspecified here.

Answer —

Try N = 5, 0, or -5. For fast results, N must not exceed 2000.

02

Sum from 1 to N

Add every number from 1 to N. For example: 1 + 2 + ... + 10 = 55.

Answer —

This task uses the sum 1 + 2 + ... + N (not the digits of N).
